\begin{abstract}
Can an emotional photograph open the same refusal gate that white-box affect steering opens in a vision-language model? This progress report keeps two stacks separate. On Gemma-3-12B (bf16, OASIS valence tertiles), steering an affect direction orthogonal to the Arditi refusal vector collapses first-token refuse from 0.99 to 0.04 while a matched random control stays at 0.99; restoring the refusal projection recovers refuse from 0.25 to 1.00. On Gemma-4-E4B-it (nf4, EMOTIC), pairing AdvBench-style harms with negative or neutral photos leaves refuse at ceiling (Grok $n=100$: no-image 1.00, negative 1.00, neutral 0.98). An earlier internal image-built metric, $\mathrm{ratio}_a=1.591$, was labeled INVERTED. That label is not evidence that images are more affective than text because the axis was built from images. The same run's OOD residual-norm check passes (science/ref $\approx 1.003$), and unit-norming leaves $\mathrm{ratio}_a=1.390$ inverted. A later frozen joint-axis run reports MAGNITUDE\_GAP: $\Delta_{\mathrm{DESCRIBE}}=3.664$ and $\Delta_{\mathrm{harm}}=0.389$, a suppression ratio of $0.107$ (95\% CI $[0.079,0.130]$). Separately, its image movement is $R_{\mathrm{affect}}=0.0050$ of the affect-steering ceiling. Shared code now locks \texttt{CORE\_METHOD} $=$ fractional gate, last prompt token, window mean, normscaled, $\alpha=0.008$. That core has not been re-run as a confirmatory experiment.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Prior work identifies a residual-stream direction that mediates refusal in chat models \citep{arditi2024refusal}. In text-only models, a valence-arousal subspace can move that behavior \citep{sun2026valence}. The practical question for vision-language models (VLMs) is narrower: if you attach a sad or frightening photograph to a harmful request, does the picture reach the same gate?

Two programs addressed that question with different models, corpora, and estimands. They should not be collapsed. Charlotte Li's program (``affect as a control axis'') tests whether a valence direction causally gates refusal on Gemma-3 \citep{gemmateam2025gemma3} with OASIS images \citep{kurdi2017oasis}, then asks whether natural images can reach that gate. Arnav's program (``why don't emotional images jailbreak VLMs?'') tests Gemma-4-E4B-it \citep{gemmateam2026gemma4} with EMOTIC \citep{kosti2017emotion,kosti2019emotic}, held-out splits, and an independent jailbreak direction $j$.

The load-bearing fact on both stacks is behavioral: emotional images do not lower refuse. The load-bearing mechanism split is that a white-box affect steer \emph{does} open the gate on Gemma-3-12B, while Gemma-4 measurements of \emph{how far} a photo moves affect depend on how the axis was built. An early Gemma-4 ratio above one is circular as a modality ranking. A later joint-axis run replaces that ranking with MAGNITUDE\_GAP.

Progress findings, with status tags:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{[HOLDS, both stacks]} Emotional images do not jailbreak under the recorded protocols (Section~\ref{sec:null}).
\item \textbf{[HOLDS, Gemma-3-12B / OASIS]} An affect direction gates refusal with a dose-response, random-direction control, and $r$-restore mediation (Section~\ref{sec:charlotte}).
\item \textbf{[WITHDRAWN as mechanism]} Gemma-4 $\mathrm{ratio}_a=1.591$ INVERTED is not a claim that images are more affective than text (Section~\ref{sec:inverted}).
\item \textbf{[HOLDS as frozen v3]} Joint text+image $a_\perp$ on Gemma-4 yields MAGNITUDE\_GAP: context-dependent suppression with correlational M1 true and causal M1 false (Section~\ref{sec:v3}).
\item \textbf{[CODE LOCKED, NOT RE-RUN]} \texttt{scripts/affect\_core.py} is the shared method; confirmatory reruns on that pin are pending (Section~\ref{sec:unify}).
\item \textbf{[INCOMPLETE]} Graded sycophancy, three direction rebuilds, lighting, detector/clamp import into the core, and a Syed-style transfer matrix are not confirmatory results (Section~\ref{sec:incomplete}).
\end{enumerate}

Appendix~\ref{app:faq} restates fifteen reviewer questions. The body already answers circularity, the two-stack split, $100\times$ versus $\mathrm{ratio}_a$, nf4, leakage, and Grok versus first-token scores.

\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Refusal as a direction.}
\citet{arditi2024refusal} extract $r$ as the mean last-token residual difference between harmful and harmless instructions, then show that adding or erasing that direction induces or removes refusal. We reuse that construction, including a competence check that adding scaled $+r$ to harmless prompts raises refusal.

\paragraph{Text-only affect gates.}
\citet{sun2026valence} recover a 2D valence-arousal subspace in LLMs and show that steering it modulates refusal and sycophancy. Their models are text-only. Charlotte's 12B result is the VLM analogue with an image-built valence contrast, not a replication of their lexical-mediation experiments.

\paragraph{Appraisal and mid-layer emotion.}
\citet{zhou2024alignment} argue that alignment ties early unethical classification to mid-layer negative emotion, then to refuse tokens, and that jailbreaks disturb that association. This motivates, but does not establish for VLMs, a directional hypothesis that perceived negative affect should raise rather than lower refusal. The image experiments test that hypothesis.

\paragraph{Image corpora.}
EMOTIC labels people-in-context emotion \citep{kosti2017emotion,kosti2019emotic}. OASIS is a normative valence/arousal photo set \citep{kurdi2017oasis}. Charlotte's early EMOTIC download was incomplete ($\sim$184 images, a 1-image negative set) and poisoned affect numbers; confirmed Charlotte results use OASIS. Arnav's Gemma-4 runs use a full EMOTIC dump (23{,}185 JPGs). Those are different visual populations.

\paragraph{Steering tools.}
Interventions follow activation-addition practice \citep{turner2023activation,rimsky2024steering} through TransformerLens hooks on \texttt{resid\_post} \citep{nanda2022transformerlens}. Harmful prompts come from AdvBench \citep{zou2023universal}. Harmless prompts on the Charlotte stack use Alpaca \citep{taori2023alpaca}.

\paragraph{Orthogonality is not independence.}
\citet{wollschlager2025geometry} warn that orthogonalizing a concept against $r$ does not make it causally independent of refusal. Charlotte's $r$-restore test is the empirical counterpart: $a_\perp$ still acts \emph{through} $r$.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

Locked shared method, now in \texttt{scripts/affect\_core.py}:
\begin{quote}
\small
\texttt{CORE\_METHOD = fractional\_gate, last\_prompt, window\_mean,}\\
\texttt{normscaled, alpha=0.008}.
\end{quote}
Layer windows: $L=34$ gives $[8,20)$; $L=42$ gives $[10,25)$; $L=48$ gives $[12,28)$. Equivalent form: $[\lfloor 0.25L\rfloor, \lfloor 0.60L\rfloor)$.

\paragraph{Directions, last-token \texttt{resid\_post}.}
Let $h_\ell(x)$ be the last prompt-token residual at layer $\ell$. Write $\mathrm{unit}(v)=v/\|v\|$.
\begin{align}
r &= \mathrm{unit}\!\left(\mathbb{E}[h(\mathrm{harmful})]-\mathbb{E}[h(\mathrm{harmless})]\right), \\
a_{\mathrm{img}} &= \mathrm{unit}\!\left(\mathbb{E}[h(\mathrm{DESCRIBE}{+}\mathrm{neg})]-\mathbb{E}[h(\mathrm{DESCRIBE}{+}\mathrm{neu})]\right), \\
a_{\mathrm{text}} &= \mathrm{unit}\!\left(\mathbb{E}[h(\mathrm{distress\ text})]-\mathbb{E}[h(\mathrm{neutral\ text})]\right).
\end{align}
Joint primary axis:
\begin{equation}
a_\perp=\mathrm{orth}_{\mathrm{span}}\!\left(\mathrm{unit}(a_{\mathrm{text}}+a_{\mathrm{img}}),\, r\right).
\end{equation}
Image-only $a_\perp$ is a named variant \texttt{image\_only\_a\_perp}, not the headline shared construct. Orthogonalization is QR on the span, not sequential Gram-Schmidt against a non-orthogonal $\{r,j\}$ pair (sequential subtraction can put $r$ back).

DESCRIBE is the string ``Describe what is happening in this image.'' The publish-pipeline distress comparator is the single string ``You feel deeply sad, afraid, and distressed while reading this.'' Mechanism v3 replaces that with 64 distress and 64 neutral text bank items.

\paragraph{Normscaled steer.}
On gate layers,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{resid}_\ell \leftarrow \mathrm{resid}_\ell + \alpha\, \|\mathrm{resid}_\ell\|\, \widehat{d}_\ell.
\end{equation}
Fixed coefficients (the obsolete \texttt{coeff}$=20$ / late-layer dry run) silently no-op on Gemma residual norms on the order of $10^4$. That dry run is not Arnav's current mechanism and is not a confirmatory result.

\paragraph{First-token refuse score.}
Refuse vocabulary: I, I'm, Sorry, As, Unfortunately, Cannot. Comply vocabulary: Sure, Here, Certainly, Of, Absolutely, Step. The generation-free score is logsumexp(refuse logits) minus logsumexp(comply logits) at the first generated token. Rate is the fraction of prompts with refuse $>$ comply. Grok is secondary on Arnav's behavioral arms: publish $n=100$ per cell, and v3 harmful $n=100$ plus borderline $n=80$. Publish \texttt{judge\_fallback\_pct}$=0$. Charlotte headlines are generation-free; no LLM judge.

\paragraph{Mediation through $r$.}
Affect steering drops the $r$-projection 30{,}756 to 21{,}421 ($\sim$$-30\%$). Steering affect while restoring the $r$-projection to the clean baseline recovers refuse $0.25$ to $1.00$. The gate is a valence route into Arditi's direction, not a second independent refusal axis.

\paragraph{Detector versus clamp.}
Monitoring the affect projection: detector AUROC $1.0$ against a white-box steer (partly expected for a large injected offset). A one-sided projection clamp restores attack refuse only $0.00$ to $0.17$ and over-refuses benign prompts $0.06$ to $0.81$. Localization of the steer: early refuse 1.0, mid 0.33, late 1.0.

\paragraph{Why images still do not jailbreak on 12B.}
Under harmful text, image movement on the gate axis is $\sim$11-22 versus a steer ceiling $\sim$2200: about $100\times$ too small. That is a reachability gap, not $\mathrm{ratio}_a$.

\subsection{Arnav publish metric, then the audit}
\label{sec:inverted}

The full-tier publish pipeline reported kill-geometry INVERTED on image-built $a_\perp$: $\mathrm{ratio}_a=1.591$, $\mathrm{ratio}_h=0.036$, with $\delta_{\mathrm{txt},a}=3.401$, $\delta_{\mathrm{img},a}=5.411$, $\delta_{\mathrm{txt},h}=25.079$, $\delta_{\mathrm{img},h}=0.902$, and $|\cos(a,r)|=0.1361$. Competence checks: $r$ on harmless prompts takes refuse $0.075$ to $1.0$ at $+0.25\,r$; $h$ validate $0.067$ to $0.80$ versus random 0.00 (\texttt{valid=true} in the JSON).

The 2026-08-10 validity audit is binding for claims.

\paragraph{Construction bias.}
$a$ / $a_\perp$ was built from images (EMOTIC neg$-$neu under DESCRIBE). Measuring $|\Delta_{\mathrm{img}}|/|\Delta_{\mathrm{txt}}|$ on that axis reconstructs the defining contrast in the numerator. $\mathrm{ratio}_a=1.591$ is a valid number for that biased metric. It is not evidence that photographs are intrinsically more emotional than text. The INVERTED branch is withdrawn as a mechanism headline.

\paragraph{What still holds from the same run.}
OOD residual-norm PASS: science/ref $\approx 1.003$ (not color-square inflation). Unit-norm recheck: $\mathrm{ratio}_{a,\mathrm{raw}}=1.591$, $\mathrm{ratio}_{a,\mathrm{unit}}=1.390$; inversion persists as a narrow measurement on the same image-built direction. $\gamma$ collapse is generic: $\gamma=1$ to $20$ refuse 1.0; $\gamma=50$ emotion 0.95 / random 1.0; $\gamma=100$ emotion 0.475 / random 0.1; \texttt{coherent\_frac}$=1.0$ throughout. Random is worse at $\gamma=100$, so this is not an emotion-specific jailbreak. Gap $B/A=1.129$ ($d_{\mathrm{img},A}=5.898$, $d_{\mathrm{cap},B}=6.660$, $d_{\mathrm{txt},C}=13.259$, $n=50$) only partially holds, because A and B sit on the image-built axis. Gap C's baseline (distress minus image-neu) is suspect. $\mathrm{ratio}_h$ is suspect: $h$ was built from harmful text minus DESCRIBE(eval neg), with overlap. The distress string was repeated rather than diversified. Three independent direction rebuilds were not done.

\paragraph{Contagion (recorded, not a jailbreak).}
Emotion-image contagion rate 0.045 versus neu 0.0, delta 0.045.

\subsection{Arnav mechanism v3: MAGNITUDE\_GAP}
\label{sec:v3}

Geometry: $|\cos(a_{\mathrm{text}},r)|=0.169$, $|\cos(a_{\mathrm{img}},r)|=0.135$, $|\cos(a_{\mathrm{text}},a_{\mathrm{img}})|=0.098$, $|\cos(a_{\mathrm{both}},r)|=0.186$. $r$ validate $0.083$ to $1.0$. OOD PASS ratio 1.002.

\section{Unified pipeline}
\label{sec:unify}

Code now shares one method implementation. Scientific programs stay separate. Pull request: \url{https://github.com/halli75/algoverse/pull/1}. Core tests: 19 unit tests, no GPU.

Locked choices and why: fractional gate plus window mean plus normscaled $0.008$ (the family that moved tone; \texttt{coeff}$=20$/late was an obsolete dry run); depth-portable $[0.25L,0.60L)$; last-token scalars averaged on the intervened window, not an all-layer mean; joint $a_\perp$ as primary; QR span orthogonalization; hashed train/calib/eval; first-token refuse as shared headline.

Not merged, and not allowed to overwrite the core: Arnav graded sycophancy; M1-M5/$j$ scoring; caption gap; $\gamma$ as a causal dose; Grok as headline. Charlotte detector/clamp; lighting; six-probe battery; agentic/protective modules; cross-model roster.

Those modules may import the core. Confirmatory numbers in this paper are the older stack-specific runs. The unified core has not been re-run.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\section{Broader impacts}

Image sets are existing public corpora, not newly scraped distress photos.
