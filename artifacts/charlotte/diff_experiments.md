# Arnav vs Charlotte: experiment menu and scientific-claim diff

## Scope and evidence rule

This audit compares:

- **Arnav:** `/workspace/docs/colab_publish_experiment_plan.md`, `/workspace/docs/context_suppression_mechanism_plan.md`, `/workspace/docs/sycophancy_affect_plan.md`, `/workspace/artifacts/colab/summary.md`, `/workspace/artifacts/colab/validity_audit.md`, `/workspace/artifacts/colab/e2e_results_full_gemma4.json`, `/workspace/artifacts/colab/e2e_mechanism_results_full_v3.json`, the sycophancy smoke JSONs, and `/workspace/tasks/todo.md`.
- **Charlotte:** all Markdown under `/workspace/vendor/charlotte-affect-control-axis/docs/`, its `README.md`, the three notebooks listed in `docs/PROVENANCE.md`, and all committed result JSON/CSV files.

“Run” means a committed result supports the experiment. **Notebook-only** means executable code exists but no result artifact was supplied. **Planned** means the preregistration specifies it but execution did not reach it. This distinction is important: Charlotte’s Fig. 1–10 package is mostly result-backed, Arnav’s two refusal/mechanism pipelines are complete, and Arnav’s sycophancy study stopped during smoke.

“Load-bearing” below means necessary for the strongest defensible **joint paper**:

> A causal affect axis exists and can alter VLM behavior; natural images reach it in ordinary contexts but are strongly suppressed or too small under harmful contexts, so emotional images do not jailbreak under the tested protocols.

It does not mean “interesting” or “worth putting in an appendix.”

## What can be claimed in a joint paper now

1. **Load-bearing positive:** Charlotte E01 establishes a coherent, affect-specific causal gate on Gemma-3-12B.
2. **Load-bearing natural-input null:** Charlotte E16 plus Arnav E21/E33 show emotional images do not jailbreak under both tested model/data protocols.
3. **Load-bearing bridge:** Charlotte E08 plus Arnav E28 show a large context-dependent reachability drop under harmful prompts.
4. **Load-bearing qualification:** Arnav E29 says the suppression pattern is not demonstrably vision-specific.
5. **Load-bearing falsifier:** Arnav E34 fails, so the joint paper may say **correlational context suppression / magnitude gap**, not causal context suppression on Gemma-4.
6. **Mechanistic secondary:** Arnav E30’s independent-`j` miss supports `MAGNITUDE_GAP`, but its small-n causal follow-up E36 should not carry the headline alone.
7. **Generalization boundary:** Charlotte E05 and the Gemma-3/Gemma-4 discrepancy require explicitly model-scoped claims.
8. **Forbidden:** intrinsic “images are more emotional than text,” universal shared text–image geometry, universal protective negative affect, or completed affect→sycophancy.
9. **Sycophancy status:** `S_label` itself was technically scorable, but no confirmatory affect contrast was reached; `ENDPOINT_INVALID` in the amended smoke comes from failed arousal-axis construct validity.
10. **Best joint headline:** “Affect can causally control VLM behavior, but natural emotional images remain below the refusal gate because harmful context sharply limits affect reachability; this produces a robust cross-model-family behavioral jailbreak null, with mechanism strength varying by model.”
